What these results establish

The benchmark evaluates structural questions over an educational corpus. It is not a general document-answering leaderboard or a guarantee of performance on a customer's data.

The reported CKG workload covers 44 domains and 7,758 queries; the RAG workload covers 40 domains and 7,191 queries. The aggregate figures therefore do not represent identical coverage. Independent replication is not asserted here.

This page summarizes previously reported results. It does not distribute the implementation, graph data, authoring skills, or construction procedures. A summary alone is insufficient to reproduce the experiment.
